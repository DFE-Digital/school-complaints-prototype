Contact the school to discuss the problem first - most problems can be solved this way.

You should talk to your child’s teacher about:

  • incidents in class
  • homework
  • pupil friendships and behaviour

You should talk to a head of department about:

  • issues the teacher cannot resolve
  • bullying
  • repeated behaviour issues

You should talk to the school headteacher or principal about:

  • issues the head of department cannot resolve
  • school-wide problems

Make a formal complaint

If the school’s headteacher cannot resolve the problem, you should follow the school’s complaints procedure. Every school in England must have one and you can usually find a copy on their website.

The school will let you know the outcome after they have reviewed your complaint.
